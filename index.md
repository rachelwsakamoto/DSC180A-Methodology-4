**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?
We have mainly been looking at how GenAI usage has been allowed in classrooms. One of the first things we look at was a research paper where 98 syllabi from 2023-2024 were collected and broken down into their AI policies. Although 2023-2024 was only a short time ago, many of the professors were apprehensive about AI usage in their classroom. That is very interesting because in my current classes, I believe that AI usage is more accepted as it could be used as a tool for learning.
